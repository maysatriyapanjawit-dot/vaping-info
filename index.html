By Maysa, Jason and Oliver

Vaping is the act of breathing in an aerosol (mist or vapour) that is created by a battery-powered device. Vapes have many harmful health effects, most of which impact the lungs through the chemicals in the aerosol, and they also carry a risk of nicotine toxicity.
